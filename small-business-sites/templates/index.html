The four styles

  • Classic
    Navy and amber. Familiar, steady and trustworthy.
  • Bold
    Black and safety yellow with heavy capitals and square corners. Loud and practical, like a van sign.
  • Clean
    Light, airy and modern with a teal accent and rounded buttons. Suits a tidy, premium feel.
  • Warm
    Cream and forest green with serif headings. Friendly, like a family firm that has been around for years.
