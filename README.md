# Weekly Money Planner

A one page budgeting tool for working out how much to put aside each week.

Enter your weekly pay or pension, your weekly bills and your monthly bills. The planner shows:

- how much to put aside each week to cover every bill
- how much is left to spend each week
- a plain warning if bills come to more than what comes in

Monthly bills are spread over 52 weeks a year (monthly total × 12 ÷ 52), so the weekly amount always covers them even in five week months.

## Privacy

No account, no name, no server. Figures are saved only in the browser on the device being used. Clearing the browser's site data removes them.

## Using it

Open `index.html` in any browser. To give someone a link, turn on GitHub Pages for this repository (Settings → Pages → deploy from the main branch) and bookmark the address on their phone or tablet home screen.
