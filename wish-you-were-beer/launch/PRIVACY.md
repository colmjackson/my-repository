# Privacy policy (draft)

This is a plain language draft for you to review. It is not legal advice. If you add the price log in `PRICE_LOG.md`, this text must change before you ship it.

---

**Wish You Were Beer: privacy policy**

Last updated: [date]

**What the app collects.** Nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. We do not run a server that receives your photos or details.

**Your camera and photos.** The app asks for camera access so you can take a photo, or you can choose a photo from your library. The photo is processed on your phone. Details you add to the image, such as a pub name, text, the time and a price, are drawn into the picture on your phone.

**Location.** The app does not read your location. The venue is whatever you type.

**Photo information.** The finished image is redrawn from scratch, so hidden information stored in the original photo, such as GPS coordinates, is not carried over.

**What is stored on your phone.** The app remembers the last pub name, the line of text, the currency and the look you chose, so you do not have to type them again. This stays in the app's own storage on your phone. Clearing the app's data removes it. If you use the web version, the app files are also kept in your browser's cache so it can work offline.

**Sharing.** The app only shares a picture when you press Share, Save or Copy caption. Share hands the picture to the app you choose, such as Instagram, Threads, X or WhatsApp. What happens to it after that is governed by that app's own policy.

**Who the app is for.** The app is intended for adults of legal drinking age and is not directed at children.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the date above will change and, for any change that affects your data, the app will tell you.

**Contact.** [support email address]

## Notes for you

1. App Store Connect asks you to complete a privacy "nutrition label". For the app as it stands, you can declare that no data is collected. Check that every plugin you add at Stage 1 does not collect anything, because the label must cover plugins as well as your own code.
2. If you use a plain hosting page for this policy, make sure it stays online. A broken link can block an update.
