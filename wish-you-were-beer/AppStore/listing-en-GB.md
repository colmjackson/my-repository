## Description (max 4000)
Wish you were beer. Photograph your drink, stamp it with where you are, the time and what it cost, and send it to the friend who should have been there.

SNAP
Take the photo in the app, or choose one from your library. A golden ratio grid shows where the glass should sit, so even a shaky pub snap ends up looking composed.

TAG
• Where: type the pub, bar or restaurant.
• Say something: keep "Wish you were beer" or write your own words.
• When: the date and time are added for you.
• How much: switch on the price and it appears as a stamp in the corner, in €, £ or $. Irish pints deserve a record.

DONE
• Five looks: Stout, Lager, Pale, Last Orders and Snug.
• Three sizes: portrait, square and story.
• Share to Instagram, Threads, X, WhatsApp or anywhere else on your phone.
Everything is drawn into the picture, so there is nothing to type or paste afterwards.

PRIVATE
No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Nothing leaves your phone until you share it.

ACCESSIBLE
VoiceOver labels and support for larger text.

FOR ADULTS
Wish You Were Beer is for people of legal drinking age. Please drink responsibly.
