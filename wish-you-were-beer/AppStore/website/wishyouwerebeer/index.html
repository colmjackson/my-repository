Wish You Were Beer

Postcards from the pub

Photograph your drink, stamp it with where you are, the time and what it cost, and send it to the friend who should have been there. Everything is drawn into the picture, so there is nothing to type or paste afterwards.

Coming soon to the App Store Free

Try it in your browser (works best on a phone; add it to your Home Screen)

Highlights

  • A golden ratio grid to line up the glass, so a quick snap looks composed
  • Five looks: Stout, Lager, Pale, Last Orders and Snug
  • The pub, your own words, the time and the price drawn into the picture
  • Portrait, square or story, shared to Instagram, Threads, X, WhatsApp and more
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Nothing leaves your phone until you share
