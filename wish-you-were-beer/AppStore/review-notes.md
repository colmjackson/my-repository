# Wish You Were Beer: notes for App Review

Paste the section below into App Store Connect > App Review Information > Notes.

---

Thank you for reviewing Wish You Were Beer. No sign-in is needed.

BASICS
- The camera starts on launch. Point it at any drink (or anything at all) and tap the round shutter. You can also tap "Choose photo" to use one from your library.
- After the photo, choose one of five looks, type where you are, and optionally switch on "Add the price". The pub name, your words, the time and the price are drawn into the picture.
- Tap Share to send the finished picture through the system share sheet.
- "Size and grid" at the bottom changes the picture size (portrait, square, story) and hides the golden ratio grid.

ALCOHOL
- The app is a camera and sharing tool. It does not sell, deliver, order, review or advertise alcohol, does not link to any brand, and has no purchases, ads, accounts or feed.
- It is intended for adults of legal drinking age, and the age rating reflects the alcohol theme.

PRIVACY
- No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking. The only permission is the camera. Nothing is sent to any server.
