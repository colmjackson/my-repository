How it works

Take a photo of your drink, or choose one from your library. Type where you are, add your own words if you like, switch on the price if you want it shown, then pick a look and press Share. Everything is drawn into the picture, so there is nothing to type afterwards.

The camera does not start

Allow camera access. On an iPhone, open Settings, then Wish You Were Beer, then turn Camera on. In Safari, open Settings, then Safari, then Camera, and allow it for this site. You can always choose a photo from your library instead.

The pub name is cut short

Long names shrink to fit, then the pub name is shortened and your own words are kept. A shorter name always reads best.

Which sizes are there

Portrait (4:5) is the default and suits a normal phone photo. Square and Story are under Size and grid. Story is 9:16 and trims the sides of a photo to fit.
