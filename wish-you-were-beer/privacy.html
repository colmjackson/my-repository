Wish you were beer

Privacy policy. Last updated 24 October 2026.

What the app collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. We do not run a server that receives your photos or details.

Your camera and photos

The app asks for camera access so you can take a photo, or you can choose a photo from your library. The photo is processed on your phone. Details you add, such as a pub name, your own words, the time and a price, are drawn into the picture on your phone.

Location

The app does not read your location. The venue is whatever you type.

Photo information

The finished image is redrawn from scratch, so hidden information stored in the original photo, such as GPS coordinates, is not carried over.

What is stored on your phone

The app remembers the last pub name, the line of text, the currency and the look you chose, so you do not have to type them again. This stays in the app's own storage on your phone, and clearing the app's data removes it. The web version also keeps its files in your browser's cache so it can work offline.

Sharing

The app only shares a picture when you press Share or Save. Share hands the picture to the app you choose, such as Instagram, Threads, X or WhatsApp. What happens to it after that is governed by that app's own policy.

Who the app is for

The app is intended for adults of legal drinking age and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change and, for any change that affects your data, the app will tell you.
